🛠 Teacher tool: add a new unit

Turn a spreadsheet into a unit

  1. In Excel or Google Sheets, make columns in this order:
    word | part of speech | Uzbek | Russian | English definition | example sentence
    (Uzbek and Russian can be empty.)
  2. Select the rows (without the header), copy them, and paste them below.
  3. Type the unit title, press the button, and copy the result.
  4. Open words.js, find the right level, and paste the result inside its units: [ ... ] list.
